## Game Setup

1. The game suits 1 to 6 players.
2. Players play to a set number of points. The default is 10,000. This is the Winning Score.
3. Each player uses eight six-sided dice.
4. Each player must reach a minimum number of points before banking any. The default is 1,000. This is the Minimum Bank. Players can change it.

## Scoring

- Three dice showing 1 are worth 1,000 points.
- Three dice showing 2 to 6 are worth 100 times the face value. Three 5s are worth 500.
- A single 1 is worth 100 points.
- A single 5 is worth 50 points.
- A fourth die that matches a three-of-a-kind of 2, 3, 4, or 6 is worth 50 points.
- Any other die is worth zero.
- Points are not saved until the player ends the turn. If a roll scores nothing, the points in your hand are lost.

## Gameplay

1. Each player rolls one die. The highest roll goes first. Ties roll again.
2. The starting player rolls all eight dice.
3. To keep rolling, your hand must score more than zero.
4. If you have reached the Minimum Bank and your hand scores, you may stop and bank.
5. If you have not reached the Minimum Bank, you must roll again.
6. Before rolling again, hold one or more scoring dice. Then roll the rest.
7. If you hold seven dice and the eighth scores, you may roll all eight again by saying "And Rolling".
8. After a bust or a bank, the dice pass to the next player clockwise.
9. Play continues until a player reaches the Winning Score. That player wins.
10. If everyone agrees, play on to the end of the round.
